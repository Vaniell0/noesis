## Corpus post-mortem (informed by Step 7 results)

Step 6 mixed action chains with ToolBench. Step 7 dropped ToolBench and ran
action chains only — and achieved 4/48 = **8.3%** with an unexpected finding:
the model began producing `<think>…</think>` spans spontaneously, without
explicit supervision for that format.

**ToolBench mixing issue:** ToolBench G123-DFS uses text-based ReAct format
(`Thought: … \n Action: … \n Action Input: …`). This is a different format
from the Anthropic tool_use action chains. The 13% supervised ratio dilutes
the action-chain signal without adding orthogonal reasoning capability.
Step 6's mixed corpus was structurally noisier than Step 7's pure corpus.

**Action-chain quality:** Opus session traces are reflexive multi-step sequences
(10–70 `tool_use` / session), directly exercising WKV state accumulation.
This is the correct direction — confirmed by Step 7 `<think>` autodiscovery.

**Conclusion:** Step 6 was a useful exploration that revealed the ToolBench
mixing problem. The cleaner Step 7 design (Opus traces only) validated the
action-chain direction. Step 6's partial epoch likely moved the model toward
the Step 7 starting point.
