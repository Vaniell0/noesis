## Key findings

1. **Architecture dominates scale effect**: G1h 2.9B base K=8,P=1 = 53% vs G1d 0.4B = ~11%.
   G1h is specifically trained for state-as-computation (G1 series); G1d is smaller but same series.

2. **Previous H12b "NO CONTAMINATION" result was P=1 artefact**:
   Quick run 2026-08-07 used P=1 only (5 probes/slot). At P=1, step7 ≈ base at K=8 (51% vs 53%).
   At P=2+, step7 degrades faster than base: K=8,P=2: step7=11% vs base=21%.

3. **Action-chain training (step7) does NOT improve multi-slot retention**:
   Step7 is worse than G1h base at K=2,P=1 (25% vs 65%) and K=4,P=1 (45% vs 67%).
   Exception: K=8,P=4 — step7=8% vs base=5% (negligible, within noise).

4. **Step7 training changed slot-access pattern**: model became better at shallow retrieval
   in high-K context (P=1 style, matching action-chain one-fact-per-turn structure)
   but lost depth retention (P=4 in low-K contexts regressed significantly at K=2).
