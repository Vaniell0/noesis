## H7. Training a skill moves facts between classes; new facts come only from context

Rewritten 2026-11-11. Notation is the frame's (`hypotheses/README.md` §2).

### 1. Objects

- *Fact classes* of an item at `M = 0`: **read** (the greedy answer is right); **stored, not read**
  (wrong, but the gold ranks high under teacher forcing or appears among samples); **lost** (neither).
  Estimators: `experiments/rl/m0_pass_probe.py --m0-rank`, `--m0-samples`.
- *Where the answer lives*: the item sets of `HZLK.md` §6 are split into **context** (the answer is in
  the given passage), **question** (answerable from the weights alone) and **two-hop** (one fact from
  each). H19 gives the estimator that tells the source apart for any item.
- *Transition table*: the classes of the same items before and after a training stage.
- *Skill stage*: a stage whose data adds no facts the model lacks: self-distillation, a hint read by the
  same model, reward.

### 2. Statement

A skill stage is a repair: its gains on items answerable from the weights come from the class
"stored, not read", and it can be run so that it loses fewer read items than it gains. Facts the
model does not hold arrive only through the context; what the stage teaches is to read them there and
combine them with what the weights hold.

This is the form of "knowledge and skills apart" that the programme acts on. The weights hold facts
and skills together and cannot be split; what can be measured is which way a stage moves facts.

*Proved part* (input bound, frame §3): a stage whose data carries no new facts cannot add one; a
reward of `|R|` outcomes carries at most `log₂|R|` bits per episode. A teacher that reads a hint passes
on how to read, and the facts of that hint only.

**Estimator.** The transition table on a fixed item set, per path (context, question, two-hop), paired by
item, before and after the stage.

**Refuter.** On items answerable from the weights alone, transitions read → lost outnumber stored, not
read → read (McNemar `p < 0.05`).

### 3. Measured

Not yet as a transition table. What exists:

- *Per-path counts after a skill stage* (Qwen3.5 0.8B, privileged-hint self-distillation at `M = 0`,
  `experiments/rl/results/qwen35_08b/`, `HZLK.md` §6 point 10): base 104 of 289 (context 87, question 17,
  two-hop 0) → 195 (84, 35, 76); 86 of the 104 previously right items kept. Weight-answerable items
  doubled and two-hop answers appeared, context reading stayed flat, 18 right items were lost. Its errors
  on two-hop items are the country (no hop) or the right capital misspelt, never another person's
  capital: the hop is real. The same procedure on G1d 0.4B, which does not hold the capitals, stays at
  0-2 of 120 two-hop items: the stage cannot supply what the weights lack.
- *A training round at scale* (UncheatableEval 2026-07, public raw files): G1h → G1L improves the mean
  compression rate at every size and worsens news and non-English encyclopedia text, by +0.105 and +0.101
  at 1.5B down to +0.018 and +0.012 at 13.3B. The cost of new skills lands on stored knowledge and
  shrinks with size.
- *State against weights* (external, BlinkDL/RWKV-LM issue #338): on World 2.9B, LoRA recalls injected
  identity facts about 100%, state tuning about 30%; a rank-32 LoRA holds 94% of the update's energy. A
  trained state changes form and dispositions, not facts.
- *A disposition installed by data* (external, Scarletwolf 2026-08-11, pre-registered): 160 examples
  filling a gap in tool-calling data cut missed abstentions 18 → 7 (`p` 0.013 at 2.9B, 0.002 at 7.2B),
  lost no legitimate call, and left name confusions flat.
- *Skill training that overwrote reading*: a tool-call fine-tune (step 8) answered every extraction task
  with a tool call (0% everywhere, `H10.md` §4); an action-chain fine-tune (step 7) lost multi-track
  retention on 7 of 9 cells (`H12.md` §3); after 200 steps of tick training, everyday questions at `M = 0`
  loop or slip into a tool-call template (`HZLK.md` §6).

**Status.** Proposed. The refuter needs the transition table; `m0_pass_probe.py` produces the classes,
and the item sets already carry the context / question / two-hop split.

### 4. History

- 2026-07 written as a design lock for the runtime: reasoning competence in the weights, fresh knowledge
  through retrieval and tool observations, judged to beat a same-size model with domain data baked in;
  predicted on the old rubric set with retrieval on and off after the planned fine-tune A1; the A3 corpus
  decision was to depend on it. Never run. Principle P2 in `docs/principles.md` states the same lock.
- 2026-10-22 the owner names the programme's training aim as teaching the model to work with knowledge
  from different sources: the weights, the input, and the latent channel that operates on both.
- 2026-11-11 restated as a claim about how a stage moves facts between classes.
