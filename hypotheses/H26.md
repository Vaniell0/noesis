## H26. Training settings select the form of a solution; for a recurrent-state model the step is best measured in the state

Record: `hypotheses/records/H26.yml`.

Rewritten 2026-11-11. Notation is the frame's (`hypotheses/README.md` §2). Every toy run below uses
`FrozenFinalReadoutController` (`experiments/A0_state_probe/micro_wkv.py`): an open-loop cell whose last
read and decay are frozen, so the open-loop lemma (`hypotheses/H25.md` §1) applies to all of them.

### 1. W — the weak statement

**Statement.** At matched held-out accuracy, the training settings (optimizer and step size) select
which solution is found. In the frozen-readout toy the solutions differ in the size of the remainder
`ρ₀ = E[y₀²] / Var(ab)`, the output left when the erase path is forced off: how large the terms are
that the erase path must undo. Every solution of that toy needs the erase path for the product.

The column recorded as "a_gate=0 ablation" in every file below is `−ρ₀` (Corollary 1 in `hypotheses/H25.md`):
an R², not a change, at most 0 for every setting of the weights. The first reading of it ("Adam
depends on the erase gate, Muon barely does") was wrong; both depend on it entirely.

**Estimator.** `ρ₀` per seed, with held-out and OOD R² and the share of seeds that converge.

**Refuters, registered.** (a) Across at least three structurally distinct tasks, Adam and Muon
solutions matched on held-out accuracy have overlapping per-seed distributions of the signature (≥ 5
seeds each, overlapping 90% intervals). (b) Confound: the shift is reproduced by a step-size change
under the same optimizer.

**Measured.**

(Muon on hidden weight matrices, AdamW elsewhere.)
A single-seed precursor and an lr sweep (`experiments/A0_state_probe/results/muon_vs_adam_toy.json`, `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/muon_vs_adam_lr_sweep.json`) fixed
Muon's lr at 0.005 before the 5-seed run; held-out R² falls as Muon's lr rises from 0.02.

Mid-training switch (`experiments/A0_state_probe/optimizer_switch_probe.py`, `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/optimizer_switch_toy.json`; 6 seeds, 16 steps,
head 16, converged runs only): mean `ρ₀` Adam 0.883, Muon 0.320, Adam then Muon 0.744, which is 25% of

goes 91% of the way. Spreads are wide (Adam ±1.01, lr ×1.5 ±2.15) and the five-fold arm converged on 3 of
6 seeds. Refuter (b) fires: a step
size change moves the signature at least as far as the change of optimizer.

**Status.** One task. Refuter (a) not run. Refuter (b) fired, so what is shown is narrower than the
optimizer's geometry: the training settings, step size included, set how large a remainder a solution
carries at equal accuracy. The premise "same loss, different solution" is established theory: steepest
descent under different norms converges to the maximisers of different margins (Adam the ℓ∞ margin,
Muon the spectral one; arXiv 2602.16340, Thm 3.5 / Cor 3.3; "Implicit Bias of Spectral Descent and
Muon", NeurIPS 2025), and the structure of the drift differs at matched validation loss (arXiv
2602.23696, without Muon). Neither asks which mechanism a solution uses.

### 2. S — the strong statement and what happened to it

The strong statement changed twice, each time after its own registered test. Its parts, the refuter
each was given before its run, and the outcome:

- **premise: no fixed weight-space norm predicts how far the state moves.** *Registered refuter:*
  `|corr(‖ΔS‖, ‖ΔW‖)| ≥ 0.9` for spectral, Frobenius or max-coordinate. *Outcome:* **survives**: 0.731 at T=4 (3
  seeds); 0.683, 0.629, 0.648 at T=4, 8, 16 on converged runs (6 seeds). *Files:* `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/step_geometry_mismatch.json`,
  `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/step_geometry_T{4,8,16,32}.json`
- **mismatch: Muon's fixed norm is what tears the state.** *Registered refuter:* (first framing). *Outcome:* **not
  supported**: Muon is the steadier optimizer in state terms, CV of `‖ΔS‖` 0.234 against Adam's 0.848. *Files:*
  `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/step_geometry_mismatch.json`
- **compounding: the decoupling grows with sequence length.** *Registered refuter:* max `|corr|` flat or rising over T
  ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32}. *Outcome:* **refuted**: flat. The per-optimizer amendment also fails (Muon 0.414 ≥ 0.3; Adam 0.648
  < 0.7); T=32 is not evaluable (Adam converges 0 of 6, already fails at T=20; Muon 1 of 6). *Files:*
  `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/step_geometry_T*.json`, `experiments/A0_state_probe/step_geometry_probe.py`
- **confound.** *Registered refuter:* an optimizer switch moves the mechanism no further than an lr change. *Outcome:*
  **fires** (§1). *Files:* `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/optimizer_switch_toy.json`
- **build / bake: some procedure raises live directions.** *Registered refuter:* none can. *Outcome:* **passes** on the
  toy: an entropy-rank term raises Muon's live directions 7.42 → 16.00, the ceiling. *Files:* `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/breadth_growth_toy.json`
- **build / bake, Goodhart.** *Registered refuter:* breadth gained without capability. *Outcome:* **fires**: quality
  −0.0138; the rescue of unstable Adam (convergence 33% → 100%) is reproduced by a rank-blind shrink of state energy
  that lowers breadth (live 6.33). *Files:* `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/breadth_growth_toy.json`
- **application against algorithm: the damage is factor-wise Muon on LoRA, not Muon.** *Registered refuter:* removed
  by a purely geometric reparametrisation. *Outcome:* **retired as a mechanism**: on the toy production factor-wise
  Muon loses retention (−0.2557) where balanced and product forms do not (−0.0003, −0.0011; Adam −0.0005), but
  stepping both factors at the large lr is catastrophic (retain R² +0.234, −747, −9892, −165 at coefficients 2.83,
  4.00, 5.66, 8.00). The step's size does the harm, not the mismatch between factors. *Files:* `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/lora_muon_toy.json`,
  `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/aspect_dose_toy.json`
- **state metric, first rung: per-tensor steps equalised in state displacement.** *Registered refuter:* no better than
  the shape-derived scale on retention and adaptation (within 0.02 R², ≥ 5 seeds). *Outcome:* not run

Breadth on real checkpoints: G1i base and the working ThinkChain LoRA (step 500) both carry 13-16 live
directions of 64 per head with the same per-head distributions (L0 3/12/26 and 3/12/26; L4 5/16/28 and
5/17/28) although their attention weights differ by 0.4-1.6% (`experiments/A0_state_probe/results/rank_recheck/jlens.json`,
`experiments/A0_state_probe/results/rank_recheck_step500/jlens.json`): a change large enough to change behaviour leaves the
spectral envelope alone. On the staged toy, plain training's share of the rank ceiling is a property of
the configuration (94% → 23-48% as the ceiling grows 3 → 16; `experiments/A0_state_probe/results/rank_ceiling_calibration.json`); in
absolute terms Adam's live directions stay near 3-4 while Muon's rise 3.00 → 7.74 (Adam's last point is
one seed). The staged lineage toy (`experiments/A0_state_probe/results/lineage_toy.json`, 6 seeds) has only the arms without LoRA adding
live directions (+0.85 Adam, +1.10 Muon), with per-seed spreads that change sign.

Real scale. Muon fine-tuning of G1i (step 500) collapsed generation quality in every configuration tried
while the training diagnostics stayed healthy (`docs/phase15-gap-matrix.md`): the motivating observation,
not a test. G1d 0.4B full fine-tuning, 30 steps, 3 data orders (`experiments/rl/results/geomgrid/`): best
held-out ΔCE equal for Muon (−0.172 at lr 3e-5) and AdamW (−0.168 at 1e-5); Muon's lr window is wider
(zero crossing near 1.4e-4 against 7.5e-5). At lr 0.02 the worst tensor moves 349% of its norm in one
step. The 2.9B collapse is the step size.

**Status.** As framed (build/bake, compounding), refuted by its own criteria. What survives is the
premise: no fixed weight-space norm predicts the state's displacement (0.648 against 0.9). A step
measured in the state, a trust region in state space rather than weight space, therefore remains a
defined and cheap next rung that has not been tried.

### 3. Settled for training

- **Step size is the damage lever.** Factor coefficients are equalised downward, never to their
  geometric mean (`experiments/rl/loader.py`): raising any factor's step is what does the harm.
- **The aspect coefficient misfires on the real model** (read from the G1i state_dict, no toy involved;
  `training/rwkv-peft-patches/README.md`): `max(1, m/n)^½` gives lora_A [32, 2560] 1.000 and lora_B
  [2560, 32] 8.944 under one shared lr; inside every FFN, `ffn.key` (10240 × 2560) 2.000 and `ffn.value`
  (2560 × 10240) 1.000. RWKV-7's own low-rank pairs (`att.a1`, `att.w1` 5.164, `att.v1` 6.325, `att.g1`
  2.828) escape Muon only because they are bare parameters, not `.weight`. The coefficient should be
  computed on the product, not on each shape.
- **Muon tolerates a wider lr window** at an equal best result and stays trainable on longer recurrences
  where Adam fails in the toy (T=20 and beyond); comparisons at long T need gradient clipping (now an
  option of `experiments/A0_state_probe/step_geometry_probe.py`), since Muon's orthogonalised update is immune to an exploding
  gradient by construction.
- **Breadth is not a lever.** Raising live directions with a loss term buys no capability (Goodhart
  fired); a stage is judged on capability and separable facts, not on breadth (`hypotheses/H12.md`).
- **ROSA parameters** (community report, RWKV Discord 2026-09-14): frozen under AdamW because
  sign-alternating gradients cancel in the first moment; they need their own lr group and a drift
  monitor per group.

### 4. Related work and the state-loss alternative

Natural gradient (Amari) takes the metric from the Fisher information; Riemannian optimisation on matrix
manifolds (Absil, Mahony, Sepulchre) moves along geodesics with retractions; unitary and orthogonal RNNs
constrain the recurrent matrix so the recurrence does not destroy gradient flow. Muon is
architecture-aware (it treats a matrix as a matrix) and state-blind. The project's trajectory loss
`L_state` pursued the same aim as a loss term and showed the failure modes of that choice: it outweighed
the task loss (step 300, `docs/verdicts/2026-08-04-a1-pilot-step3-step4.md`) and saturated its clamp into
a zero gradient (pinned at −10.0 from step 0 on the step 9b corpus, 2026-09-10). A step measured in the
state constrains the descent instead of the objective.
