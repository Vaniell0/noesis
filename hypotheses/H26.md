## H26. Training settings select the form of a solution; for a recurrent-state model the step is best measured in the state

Rewritten 2026-11-11. Notation is the frame's (`hypotheses/README.md` §2). Every toy run below uses
`FrozenFinalReadoutController` (`experiments/A0_state_probe/micro_wkv.py`): an open-loop cell whose last
read and decay are frozen, so the open-loop lemma (`H25.md` §1) applies to all of them.

### 1. W — the weak statement

**Statement.** At matched held-out accuracy, the training settings (optimizer and step size) select
which solution is found. In the frozen-readout toy the solutions differ in the size of the remainder
`ρ₀ = E[y₀²] / Var(ab)`, the output left when the erase path is forced off: how large the terms are
that the erase path must undo. Every solution of that toy needs the erase path for the product.

The column recorded as "a_gate=0 ablation" in every file below is `−ρ₀` (Corollary 1 in `H25.md`):
an R², not a change, at most 0 for every setting of the weights. The first reading of it ("Adam
depends on the erase gate, Muon barely does") was wrong; both depend on it entirely.

**Estimator.** `ρ₀` per seed, with held-out and OOD R² and the share of seeds that converge.

**Refuters, registered.** (a) Across at least three structurally distinct tasks, Adam and Muon
solutions matched on held-out accuracy have overlapping per-seed distributions of the signature (≥ 5
seeds each, overlapping 90% intervals). (b) Confound: the shift is reproduced by a step-size change
under the same optimizer.

**Measured.**

**Status.** One task. Refuter (a) not run. Refuter (b) fired, so what is shown is narrower than the
optimizer's geometry: the training settings, step size included, set how large a remainder a solution
carries at equal accuracy. The premise "same loss, different solution" is established theory: steepest
descent under different norms converges to the maximisers of different margins (Adam the ℓ∞ margin,
Muon the spectral one; arXiv 2602.16340, Thm 3.5 / Cor 3.3; "Implicit Bias of Spectral Descent and
Muon", NeurIPS 2025), and the structure of the drift differs at matched validation loss (arXiv
2602.23696, without Muon). Neither asks which mechanism a solution uses.

### 3. Settled for training

- **Step size is the damage lever.** Factor coefficients are equalised downward, never to their
  geometric mean (`experiments/rl/loader.py`): raising any factor's step is what does the harm.
- **The aspect coefficient misfires on the real model** (read from the G1i state_dict, no toy involved;
  `training/rwkv-peft-patches/README.md`): `max(1, m/n)^½` gives lora_A [32, 2560] 1.000 and lora_B
  [2560, 32] 8.944 under one shared lr; inside every FFN, `ffn.key` (10240 × 2560) 2.000 and `ffn.value`
  (2560 × 10240) 1.000. RWKV-7's own low-rank pairs (`att.a1`, `att.w1` 5.164, `att.v1` 6.325, `att.g1`
  2.828) escape Muon only because they are bare parameters, not `.weight`. The coefficient should be
  computed on the product, not on each shape.
- **Muon tolerates a wider lr window** at an equal best result and stays trainable on longer recurrences
  where Adam fails in the toy (T=20 and beyond); comparisons at long T need gradient clipping (now an
  option of `step_geometry_probe.py`), since Muon's orthogonalised update is immune to an exploding
  gradient by construction.
- **Breadth is not a lever.** Raising live directions with a loss term buys no capability (Goodhart
  fired); a stage is judged on capability and separable facts, not on breadth (`H12.md`).
- **ROSA parameters** (community report, RWKV Discord 2026-09-14): frozen under AdamW because
  sign-alternating gradients cancel in the first moment; they need their own lr group and a drift
  monitor per group.

### 4. Related work and the state-loss alternative

Natural gradient (Amari) takes the metric from the Fisher information; Riemannian optimisation on matrix
manifolds (Absil, Mahony, Sepulchre) moves along geodesics with retractions; unitary and orthogonal RNNs
constrain the recurrent matrix so the recurrence does not destroy gradient flow. Muon is
architecture-aware (it treats a matrix as a matrix) and state-blind. The project's trajectory loss
`L_state` pursued the same aim as a loss term and showed the failure modes of that choice: it outweighed
the task loss (step 300, `docs/verdicts/2026-08-04-a1-pilot-step3-step4.md`) and saturated its clamp into
a zero gradient (pinned at −10.0 from step 0 on the step 9b corpus, 2026-09-10). A step measured in the
state constrains the descent instead of the objective.
