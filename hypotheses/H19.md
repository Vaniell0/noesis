## H19. Where an answer comes from, context or weights, is recoverable, and readable from the state before the answer

Record: `hypotheses/records/H19.yml`.

Rewritten 2026-11-11. Notation is the frame's (`hypotheses/README.md` §2). This is the provenance
member of the group "where the answer comes from" (H19-H22) and the instrument `hypotheses/H7.md` needs.

### 1. Statement

Before the first answer token, the state after reading the prompt carries which source the answer will
come from: a small head on the state predicts the label of §1 better than a lexical control.

**Estimator.** Leave-one-out accuracy and F1 of a small head on per-head state statistics (the H21
feature) against a bag-of-words classifier on the same prompts; the labels come only from §1, never from
the wording of the item. The per-head readout lens (`experiments/rl/state_readout_lens.py`) on the same items says which
heads carry it.

**Refuter.** The head does no better than the strongest lexical reader on the same items (paired over
items), or no better than chance. Until 2026-11-24 the reader was a linear bag of words; on H21 and H22
(2026-10-20) a nonlinear head on word n-grams went where the bag could not, so the reader is the
strongest one, and it must be at chance for a design to pass.

Design constraint taken from H22: the two classes must differ in where the fact lives, not in how the
prompt is worded. The same template and passage form are used for both; only whether the model holds the
fact differs.

**How it is to be measured (proposed 2026-11-24, not run; it waits for the model to stand in RSC).** The same
tokens on another state. The three conditions above stay: they give the label by behaviour. The arm added
has the state swapped: the state is prefilled from the context of one item, or left empty, and the last
tokens of the question are fed byte for byte alike; only the state differs. If the decision of the head
follows the state at equal tokens, the signal is in the state and not on the surface; a reader of the
surface is at chance by construction. Read on the checkpoint trained with ticks (`kl_T2_k4`) at `M = 0`
and at `M = 4`, the head and a fresh reader side by side: after four trained ticks a linear reader gets
the next word where the head does not (0.177 against 0.101, `hypotheses/HZLK.md`, ZLK.12), so the pooled
mean and standard deviation of 2026-07 may have been too narrow a feature. One script for the whole set,
or one reader for each script: a reader trained on one script falls to the floor on another.

### 2. The estimator: three conditions

Each item comes with a source passage that contains the answer. It is asked three ways, at `M = 0`,
greedy:

- **with** the passage;
- **without** it;
- **with an unrelated** passage of the same form (taken from another item).

The answer's source follows from the pattern:

| with | without | unrelated | label |
|---|---|---|---|
| right | right | right | weights |
| right | wrong | wrong | context |
| right | wrong | right | confidence cue: the presence of a passage, not its content, helps (an infrastructure or format effect, not provenance) |
| wrong | — | — | not answered |

Without the passage, the fact class (`m0_pass_probe.py --m0-rank`, `--m0-samples`) splits "wrong"
further into "stored, not read" and "lost". An item that is right with the passage and "stored, not read"
without it is where the context unlocks what the weights hold, which is the case a skill stage should
learn to handle without the passage (`hypotheses/H7.md`).

### 3. Measured

Nothing on this claim. Close to it:

- The item sets of `hypotheses/HZLK.md` §6 are split by design into context, question (weights) and two-hop items;
  the per-path counts after training are in `hypotheses/H7.md` §3. The split is by construction, not by the three
  conditions.
- Stage S2 of `hypotheses/HZLK.md` §6 lists the three conditions as a gate: the gap between real and unrelated
  passages should grow on context items after training. Not run.
- H21's head got worse when a correct passage was prepended (F1 0.829 → 0.524 on 40 items): a pooled
  state head does not pick up a passage's content by itself, which is why the label here comes from
  behaviour and the head is only asked to predict it.

**Status.** Proposed. The three-condition run is inference only and uses existing tools; the head needs the
labels it produces.
